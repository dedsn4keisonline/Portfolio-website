HASAN(1)Personal ManualHASAN(1)

SYNOPSIS

hasan [--security] [--linux] [--machines] [--operating-system]

DESCRIPTION

Hey, I'm Hasan. I built this site to collect the tech I've worked on and messed around with.

I take systems apart, old and new, to learn how they work and why they sometimes don't. Have a look around.

OPTIONS

--security
Cybersecurity, learned hands-on through TryHackMe rooms and my own labs.
--linux
Living in the terminal, and learning the system underneath it.
--machines
Legacy hardware and software. Digging into how things used to be built.
--operating-system
The part I enjoy most. Default: on.

FILES

projects.html
Write-ups, experiments, and tools I've built.
